Sharing raw business financial data directly with external LLMs can create serious risks, especially for a fintech product like SoleBook.
For a hackathon MVP, using LLMs is fine.
But for a real fintech product, you need a privacy-first AI architecture.

Main Risks of Sending Financial Data to LLMs
1. Privacy & Confidentiality Risk
Financial records may contain:
account numbers
supplier details
salaries
transaction histories
invoices
business profits
customer information
This is highly sensitive business data.

2. Regulatory & Compliance Risk
In fintech/banking environments:
data residency matters
auditability matters
consent matters
Future compliance concerns may include:
banking regulations
PDPA/privacy laws
financial data governance
Judges from banking/compliance will definitely think about this.

3. AI Hallucination Risk
LLMs can:
misunderstand transactions
generate incorrect financial advice
misclassify business events
You should NEVER allow LLMs to:
directly move money
autonomously approve payments
become the financial source of truth

The Correct Real-World Architecture
RULE:
“LLM should never be the core financial engine.”
The LLM should be:
a conversational intelligence layer only.

Deterministic system.

financial workflows
